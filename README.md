A descriptive analysis of 614 loan applications examining factors associated with approval and rejection.

#Key Results

-Applications: 614
-Approved: 422
-Overall approval rate: 68.7%
-Good-credit approval: 79.6%
-Poor-credit approval: 7.9%
-Good vs. poor credit shows roughly a 10× difference in approval.
-Main Findings
-Credit history is the strongest factor.
-Property location has a secondary effect: Semiurban 76.8%, Urban 65.8%, Rural 61.5%.
-Income has limited influence; approval rates across income bands range from 56.5% to 72.2%.
-Graduates, married applicants and applicants with a co-applicant were approved 7–9 percentage points more often; self-employment had  little effect.
-Loan amount has only a mild relationship with approval.
